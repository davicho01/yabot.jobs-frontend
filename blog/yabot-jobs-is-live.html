yabot.jobs is live: job search straight from company career pages

David Garcia
October 23, 2026 · 2 min read

Today we're opening yabot.jobs to everyone. It's a free, open-source job search that pulls jobs straight from company career sites: over a million so far, from more than 10,000 companies, with about 30,000 new ones every weekday, in every sector. Before you apply, it tells you how well you fit, and it helps you tailor your resume for the job.

What you can do today

  • Search jobs at the source, newest first, by title, company or metro area. No sign-up needed.
  • See your fit: a score out of 100, the gaps, and the honest risks, for any job.
  • Tailor your resume in one click, then review what changed before you use it.
  • Get the rest done: cover letters, interview prep, alerts for new jobs, and a tracker for every application.

Why we built it

Job boards are full of reposts, stale listings and dates you can't trust. In our own data, nearly half of open tech and startup jobs are over two months old.

yabot.jobs reads jobs from the company's own site and keeps checking back. When a job comes down from a site we read in full, it's marked closed. And it never dates a job later than the day it first found it, so a refreshed listing can't pass as new. Nothing is ever submitted for you: you see the work, and you hit apply.

Free to start

Searching, tracking and alerts are always free, and your first 10 AI-scored jobs are free too. After that, it's $5 a month for our hosted AI, or $0 if you connect your own.

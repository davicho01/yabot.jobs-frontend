About

Built by someone who needed it too.

Yabot Jobs isn't a company with a growth plan. It's one engineer's tool, opened up for anyone else on the same job hunt.

David Garcia
Software engineer, Utah · builder of Yabot Jobs

I was born in Peru and moved to the U.S. with my family when I was 15. These days I live in Utah, where my three grown children, who I could not be prouder of, keep me honest about what actually matters. I've been a software engineer for more than 15 years.

Why I built this

I built Yabot Jobs after getting laid off. I needed a way to search for jobs, track what I'd applied to, and use modern AI tools to sharpen my resume for each posting, and I needed it to be affordable while I had no income coming in. Nothing I found did all three without charging a subscription for the privilege.

So I built the tool I wished existed. Once it was working for me, it was obvious it could help anyone else in the same spot, so I opened it up.

How it stays free

Searching the board, saving jobs, and tracking applications cost nothing, for anyone. The part that costs real money is AI: scoring, tailoring, and writing all run on a model, and model usage isn't free. Every account gets a handful of free evaluations to try all of it with no key and no card. After that, you can bring your own AI API key (Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, or any OpenAI-compatible service) and keep using every feature for free; you pay your provider directly for what you use, and I add nothing on top. If you'd rather not manage a key, there's an optional $5/month plan that covers it for you.

I'd like to offer more for free outright. The honest constraint is that AI costs money, and bring-your-own-key is how I can keep the whole thing free without running it at a loss.

The short version

  • One person builds and runs this, not a company with investors to answer to.
  • Searching, saving, and tracking jobs is free for everyone, no account required to browse.
  • Bring your own AI key and every AI feature is free of charge from me; you only pay your AI provider for what you use.
  • The code is open source, so you can see exactly what it does with your data.

Open source

Yabot Jobs is open source. You can read the code, see what's sent where, and run it yourself: frontend and backend on GitHub.

About

Job search, straight from the source.

Yabot Jobs is a job search built by Vectorwell Technologies. We collect jobs directly from company career sites, keep checking that they're still open, and give every job seeker the tools to apply well.

Why we built it

Job searching has become a second job. The same listings get reposted across a dozen sites, roles stay up long after they're filled, and the date on a posting often says when it was last refreshed, not when it opened. Then candidates rewrite their resume for every application and hear nothing back.

We built Yabot Jobs to fix the parts of that we can: real jobs from the company's own site, honest dates, and AI that helps you put your best application forward instead of sending more of them.

What we stand for

  • Searching should be free. Browsing, saving, tracking and alerts cost nothing, for anyone, with no account needed to browse.
  • Real jobs, real dates. Jobs come from company career sites, not copies on other boards, and we never show a job as newer than the day we first found it.
  • You stay in control. We never apply for you. You see every score, every change to your resume, and you hit apply.
  • Your data is yours. We don't sell it or use it to train AI models, and you can delete anything in your account at any time.

How Yabot Jobs is paid for

  • Search, tracking and alerts are free. Only the AI features cost money to run.
  • Every account gets 10 jobs of AI help free, with no card.
  • After that, it's $5 a month for up to 100 jobs on our hosted AI.
  • Or connect your own Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek or OpenAI-compatible account and pay us nothing; your provider bills you directly, and we add nothing on top.

Built in the open

Yabot Jobs is open source, so anyone can check what it does with their data: read the website code and the API code on GitHub.

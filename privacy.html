Privacy

Your resume is yours.

Last updated October 21, 2026

The short version

  • You can browse jobs without an account.
  • Your resume and notes are used to score jobs, tailor resumes, and write cover letters for you. Nothing else.
  • To do that, your resume and the job posting are sent to an AI provider.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models, and we do not sell it.
  • You can delete anything on your account at any time.
  • The code is open source, so you can read exactly what it does.

What we store

When you use Yabot Jobs with an account, we store what you add or create so the tool works:

  • Your account details: your email address, and a display name if you set one. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address and name with us, and we use that name as your display name unless you set your own.
  • For each device you are signed in on: the browser type and IP address, so we can keep sessions secure.
  • The resumes you upload and the tailored versions we create for you.
  • The jobs you save, their fit scores, and the reports behind those scores.
  • Cover letters, interview prep, notes, and follow-up reminders.
  • Your application status, such as saved, applied, or archived.
  • Your saved searches and whether you want email alerts for them.
  • Any AI API key you add, stored encrypted, so your requests can run on it.
  • If you subscribe, your Stripe customer and subscription reference. Never your card details.

Browsing jobs does not require an account.

How AI uses your resume

Scoring, tailoring, and writing all need an AI model. When you evaluate a job, your resume and that job posting are sent to an AI provider, which returns the result. Which provider handles it depends on how you use Yabot Jobs:

Hosted AIIncluded with your free jobs and the $5 plan. Currently provided by DeepSeek.
Your own AI accountYou connect Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, or any OpenAI-compatible service. Your resume goes to the provider you chose, under the terms you have with them.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to read the policy of whichever provider you use.

About the hosted AI: DeepSeek is operated from China. Its privacy policy says information may be stored on servers there and may be used to improve its services. If that does not work for you, use your own key with a provider you trust, or avoid putting sensitive details in your resume.

On our side, Yabot Jobs does not use your data to train models.

Who else handles your data

Yabot Jobs runs on services from other companies, and they process data on our behalf so the site works. We do not share your data with anyone beyond these:

  • The AI provider that processes your request, as described above.
  • Amazon Web Services: the website itself, and storage for the resume files you upload.
  • Amazon Simple Email Service: sign-in links, job alerts, and reminder emails.
  • Google: if you choose Continue with Google, Google confirms who you are. We never see your Google password and get no access to your Gmail, Drive, or anything else in your Google account.
  • Google Cloud: the API behind the site and its database.
  • Stripe: payments, if you subscribe.
  • Google Analytics: statistics about how the site is used. See below.

Cookies

When you sign in, we set one cookie that keeps you signed in. It is needed for the site to work and is removed when you sign out. The only other cookie is the analytics one described below.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see which pages people visit and which links they follow, so we know what is useful. It sets a cookie with a random ID and records the pages you view, the links you click on our job pages, your approximate location (from your IP address), and your browser and device type. Google collects this on our behalf; it is not linked to your account, and we do not use it for advertising.

  • If you visit from the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, analytics cookies are off. We only receive cookieless, anonymous counts.
  • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, analytics cookies are off for you too, wherever you are.
  • We do not respond to the older Do Not Track signal, because there is no agreed standard for it. Global Privacy Control is the signal we honor.
  • Google Signals and ad personalization are turned off, so this data is not used for ads.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your resume or notes for advertising.
  • We do not apply to jobs for you. You always apply on the company's own site.

Payments

Paid plans are processed by Stripe, on a checkout page hosted by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe, not to Yabot Jobs. We only see that a payment was made and which plan you are on.

Deleting your data

You can delete resumes, jobs, notes, cover letters, applications, saved searches, and any API key you added from your account whenever you want. If you want your whole account removed, ask us and we will delete it.

Data already sent to an AI provider is covered by that provider's retention policy, which we do not control.

Age

Yabot Jobs is for people 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a child has made an account, tell us and we will delete it.

Open source

Yabot Jobs is open source. You can read the code, see what is sent where, and run it yourself: frontend and backend on GitHub.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how your resume is processed, we will say so clearly.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: get in touch from the help page. The rules for using Yabot Jobs are in our terms of service.

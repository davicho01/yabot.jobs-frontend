How Yabot Jobs works

From finding a posting to keeping track of the interview, in the order you'd actually do it.

  1. Step 1

    Find a posting

    Search the job board by title, city, or state. When a city comes up short, Yabot Jobs tells you how far around it looked and lets you widen the search.

    Narrow things down to remote, hybrid, or on-site work, or to jobs posted in the last day, week, or month.

    Remote jobs Hybrid jobs On-site jobs Posted in the last 24 hours Last 7 days Last 30 days

    Jump straight into a filtered search.

  2. Step 2

    Check how well you fit

    Upload your resume once, as a PDF or a Word file. For any posting, Yabot Jobs compares it with what the employer is asking for and gives you a fitness report with a short explanation.

    You find out before you spend an evening on an application whether it's worth the evening.

    • Qualified

      Your resume meets what the posting asks for.

    • Maybe

      Close. A tailored resume could tip it.

    • Not a match

      There's a real gap. Save your time for a better fit.

  3. Step 3

    Tailor your resume and cover letter

    Yabot Jobs rewrites your resume for the posting in a layout applicant tracking systems can read, and drafts a cover letter that speaks to the role.

    Download either one as a Word document (.docx) and change anything you like.

    Bring your own AI key

    These tools run on a key from Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, or any OpenAI-compatible service. You pay that provider directly for what you use, and Yabot Jobs adds no fee. That's how the whole site stays free.

    .docx
  4. Step 4

    Keep track of every application

    Save a posting, then move it along as things happen: saved, applied, interviewing, offer. Rejected and withdrawn have their own places, and every application has room for your notes.

    Not every job is on Yabot Jobs, and it doesn't have to be. Found one on another job board or a company's careers page? Paste its link and Yabot Jobs reads the posting and adds it to your list, so everything you're chasing lives in one place and nothing slips through.

Search for jobs from ChatGPT or Claude.

Yabot Jobs has an MCP server. Connect it to your ChatGPT or Claude account and ask for jobs in plain language while your assistant does the searching.

  • Search the job board and open any posting for the full details
  • Read your resume and score how well it fits a posting
  • Write a tailored resume and cover letter and save them to your account as Word documents
  • Log applications as applied, then update their status and notes
  • Add a posting from its link

Your assistant does the writing and scoring itself and saves the results to your account, so you don't need to add an API key to Yabot Jobs to use it this way.

Connect it in three steps

  1. In your assistant's settings, add a custom connector (a remote MCP server) with this address: https://mcp.yabot.jobs/mcp
  2. Sign in to Yabot Jobs when your assistant opens the sign-in page. We email you a link, same as always.
  3. Approve access. Your assistant can now work with your Yabot Jobs account.

Using Claude Code? Run this instead: claude mcp add --transport http yabot-jobs https://mcp.yabot.jobs/mcp

Common questions

Anything else? The project is public on GitHub.

Is Yabot Jobs really free?

Yes. Searching the job board, reading postings, saving jobs, and tracking applications cost nothing.

The AI tools (fitness reports, tailored resumes, and cover letters) run on an API key you add yourself, so any charge comes from your AI provider for what you use. Yabot Jobs adds no fee on top.

Do I need an account to search for jobs?

No. You can search and browse the job board without one. Create a free account to save postings, track applications, upload a resume, and use the AI tools.

There's no password. Enter your email and we send a sign-in link that works for 15 minutes.

Where do the job postings come from?

Yabot Jobs scans public job postings and reads the details out of the page: title, company, location, work type, and pay. Once you're signed in, you can also add any posting by pasting its link, and Yabot Jobs scans it for you.

Can I track jobs I found on other job boards?

Yes. Once you're signed in, paste the link to a posting from another job board or a company's careers page. Yabot Jobs scans it, reads the title, company, location, work type, and pay, and adds it to your jobs. From there you can check your fit, tailor your resume, and track the application with everything else. If a page can't be read, rescan it later. Your AI assistant can add a posting from its link too.

Which AI providers can I use?

Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, or any OpenAI-compatible service. Your key is used only for your own resume reviews, scoring, and generation requests. You add it on the AI API Keys page after you sign in.

Can I edit the resume and cover letter it writes?

Yes. Download them as Word documents (.docx) and change anything you like before you send them.

Can I use Yabot Jobs from ChatGPT or Claude?

Yes. Yabot Jobs has an MCP server, so ChatGPT, Claude, and any other assistant that supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP) can connect to it. Add https://mcp.yabot.jobs/mcp as a custom connector in your assistant's settings and approve access on Yabot Jobs. Your assistant can then search postings, track applications, and upload tailored resumes and cover letters for you.

Is Yabot Jobs open source?

Yes. The website and the API behind it are both public on GitHub, and the website is released under the MIT license.
